02 / The quick answer

What is an
Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-organizing list used to collect and compare shopping finds. Useful fields include the source link, category, selected size, listed price, photo notes and the date each detail was checked.

This page is an independent guide to that workflow. It connects you with CAFANS categories and explains how to assess listings; it does not provide a downloadable sheet, live inventory or a guarantee of product quality.

04 / Oopbuy spreadsheet guide

Build a shortlist you can actually compare.

STEP 01 / DISCOVER

Separate your categories.

Keep shoes, clothing, headwear and jewelry in separate groups. An Oopbuy finds spreadsheet becomes easier to scan when each item has a clear category and source link.

STEP 02 / CHECK

Compare like with like.

For an Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet, compare the same size and variant. For an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet, record actual garment measurements, fabric details and the intended fit.

STEP 03 / PLAN

Keep unknowns visible.

Use an Oopbuy accessories spreadsheet to track dimensions, materials and unresolved questions. Mark missing information as “unconfirmed” and separate estimated costs from confirmed charges.

05 / The comparison checklist

A useful row beats a long list.

Use these fields in your own notebook or spreadsheet before making a purchase decision.

FieldWhat to recordWhy it helps
Source & variantListing link, seller, selected color and exact versionPrevents comparisons between different products or options.
Fit & measurementsFootwear size system; garment chest, length and waist; accessory dimensionsMakes size comparisons more meaningful than labels alone.
MaterialsThe listing’s stated fabric, composition, lining and hardwareShows where descriptions differ or information is missing.

07 / Straight answers

Before you
open another tab.

Quick explanations for building a more useful product shortlist.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-organizing list used to collect and compare shopping finds. Useful fields include the source link, category, selected size, listed price, photo notes and the date each detail was checked.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing and comparison guide. Its category links open CAFANS, where visitors can explore product listings.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

Compare the exact variant, measurement chart, material description, available photos and applicable return conditions. For clothing, use garment measurements rather than relying only on a size label.

Is the listed product price the total cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the transaction, shipping, service fees, currency conversion and applicable taxes or duties may add to the listed price. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.

Do brand photos or spreadsheet entries prove authenticity?

No. A photo, brand name or spreadsheet entry does not establish authenticity, seller authorization or product availability. Verify the specific seller and supporting evidence independently.

Is CAFANS an official Oopbuy or brand website?

This is an independent CAFANS guide. It does not claim affiliation with or endorsement by Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
